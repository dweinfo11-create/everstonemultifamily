Acquisition Process

How we evaluate and execute on every deal

A repeatable, process-driven approach from initial screening through close — applied consistently on every acquisition.

Step 01

Market screening

We identify markets with demonstrated rent growth, population inflow, and job diversification — then source deals within those markets through established broker relationships.

Step 02

In-house underwriting

Every deal is underwritten internally against T12 actuals, rent roll data, and current market comps. No assumptions, no projections built to impress.

Step 03

Disciplined offers

Offers are made only when returns clear our thresholds at our price. We do not pursue deals that do not meet our criteria — regardless of market pressure.

Step 04

Investor reporting

Regular reporting on all active investments — financials, operational updates, and distributions on schedule. No surprises.

About

Everstone Capital

Everstone Capital is a private real estate investment firm focused on the acquisition and repositioning of multifamily assets across select high-growth markets in the United States. Our strategy centers on identifying undervalued properties with clear operational upside — and executing on that upside through disciplined capital allocation and active asset management.

We operate with an institutional underwriting process applied to middle-market deals — the segment most institutional capital overlooks. That's where we find value, and that's where our investors benefit.
